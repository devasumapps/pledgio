Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026
The short version: Pledgio has no account and no server of its own, and it shows no ads. Your app list, usage, and pledges stay on your phone.

1. Who we are

Pledgio, for iPhone and Android, is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: devasumapps@gmail.com.

2. Information Pledgio uses on your device

To work, Pledgio uses the following information. It is stored and processed on your phone, and Pledgio does not send it to us.

  • The apps you choose to limit.
  • Your app usage, such as how long you use your chosen apps. It is used to show your progress and set your daily aim.
  • What you enter in the app, such as your pledge, your reasons for opening an app, and your plan and guard settings.

3. Permissions

Android

  • Usage Access shows Pledgio how long you use your apps.
  • Accessibility service lets Pledgio notice when an app you chose opens, so the pause can appear. It does not read what is on your screen, what you type, or your passwords. You turn it on yourself and can turn it off at any time.
  • Display over other apps (optional) lets the pause appear when the Accessibility service is off.
  • Notifications, battery and background permissions keep the pause and your reminders working.

iPhone

  • Screen Time lets Pledgio pause the apps you choose. Apple keeps your app choices private from Pledgio, and usage details stay within Apple's Screen Time system on your device.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your app list, usage, pledges or reasons on any server.
  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not require an account.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and other purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. They process your payment and may collect purchase and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Deleting your information

Your Pledgio information is on your phone, so deleting the app removes it. You can also remove any permission in your phone's settings at any time.

7. Children

Pledgio is not intended for children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: devasumapps@gmail.com
