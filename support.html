Common questions

Why doesn't the pause appear on Android? Open Pledgio and follow any prompt on Home. Check that Usage Access and the Accessibility service are on for Pledgio. Some phones also stop background apps to save battery, so allow Pledgio to run without battery limits.
Why doesn't the pause appear on iPhone? Open Pledgio and check Home. Screen Time access must stay on for Pledgio, in iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access.
Can I still open a paused app? Yes, always. Pledgio slows you down and never locks you out. Past your daily aim, the pause just takes a little longer.
What does the Accessibility service see? Only which app has opened, so the pause can appear. It does not read your screen, what you type, or your passwords.
How does the free plan work? The pause on up to 3 apps, focus sessions, and your weekly view are free. Pledgio Pro adds every app you choose, the 28-day plan, and Night and Morning Guard.
How do I cancel my subscription? Android: open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pledgio → Cancel.
iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pledgio → Cancel.
You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. After that, your top 3 apps stay paused.
How do I get a refund? Purchases are handled by the store, so refunds are too. Ask through Google Play or Apple.
How do I restore my purchase? Open the Pledgio Pro screen in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Google or Apple account you bought with.
How do I move to a new phone? Install Pledgio on the new phone, set it up again, and restore your purchase. Your plan and history stay on the old phone, because Pledgio keeps nothing on a server.
How do I stop using Pledgio? Delete the app. The pause ends with it on both iPhone and Android. Cancel any subscription as well, because deleting the app does not cancel it.
How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Your Pledgio information is on your phone, and we hold no copy on any server.
